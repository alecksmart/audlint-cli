Accessibility review — 1 high, 3 medium, 1 low

review-accessibility.md

The baseline terminal contract is adequate and ADR-0009 is honored, but one identity ambiguity and four narrower presentation/entry boundaries remain.

high

Full item identity disappears behind clipping

DESIGN.md:54,66; EXPERIENCE.md:51,140
No keyboard-reachable surface reveals the complete Current artist/year/album identity after display-cell clipping.
Fix: Add a read-only Help/detail entry that wraps or pages the full sanitized identity.
medium

Redirected and non-TTY behavior is uncommitted

EXPERIENCE.md:19,135
The contract does not prevent cursor-control output in files, split /dev/tty rendering, or blocked automation.
Fix: Require a usable controlling terminal or emit one plain stderr diagnostic and return without mutation.
medium

Busy surfaces hide the input and cancellation contract

DESIGN.md:56,69; EXPERIENCE.md:64,91,199
Measurement and sync drain input, but their surfaces do not say advertised commands such as q are inactive.
Fix: Replace inactive hints with a bounded Working — input ignored legend.
medium

Progress repaint has no reduced-motion or flicker bound

DESIGN.md:38,69; EXPERIENCE.md:91,100
Progress has no cadence cap or prohibition on blink, spinners, marquees, or full-body flashing.
Fix: Coalesce reserved-row updates and forbid blink, spinner, marquee, and alternating-color animation.
low

Inherited ANSI roles have no contrast boundary

DESIGN.md:38,42; ADR-0009:20
The limits of color behavior on operator-controlled terminal palettes are not stated.
Fix: Set no background, dim, blink, or color-only emphasis; describe NO_COLOR as escape-free presentation, not conformance.

Interaction and safety edge cases — 1 high, 4 medium

review-edge-cases.md

All ten conditions from the prior review are now closed. One cold-load ownership gap and four narrower boundary ambiguities remain.

high

Initial checked-set sizing has no input-ownership boundary

EXPERIENCE.md:68-75
Queued Space, w, q, paste, or partial terminal sequences can execute immediately after cold-load sizing completes.
Fix: Make sizing input-owning through authoritative redraw; define safe controls, drain bytes, and reset parser state.
medium

The 40–59-cell footer tier does not fit at 40–42 cells

DESIGN.md:54-60
The exact footer string is 43 cells while its range starts at 40 and fixed rows may not wrap.
Fix: Add a fitting variant, shorten it, or move the tier's lower bound to 43.
medium

Too-small q semantics conflict with preserved search and Help modes

EXPERIENCE.md:62-64,74-75,115-121
Below 12×20, q can mean query input, Help dismissal, or selector exit.
Fix: Make too-small a distinct mode with one visible q action and suspended underlying bindings.
medium

Search omits committed year matching and derived expansion restoration

.memlog.md:28,30; EXPERIENCE.md:37,43-45
The final spine drops year matching and the temporary expansion overlay committed in the decision log.
Fix: Match artist, rendered year, and album text; derive result expansion without mutating session expansion.
medium

Global size is ambiguous when checked payload is unknown

DESIGN.md:64-66; EXPERIENCE.md:59-61,72-73
Selector chrome may present known bytes as a complete total while one or more checked albums remain unknown.
Fix: Show size unknown, or label the subtotal partial and retain the unknown count.
