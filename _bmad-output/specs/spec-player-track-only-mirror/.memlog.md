topic: Player track-only exact mirror
updated: 2026-09-01T00:18
---

- (direction) Headless express creation was selected because the user supplied complete intent and requested the artifact now.
- (note) This is a follow-on contract to SPEC-player-content-filesystem-inventory; that frozen spec preserves the former payload policy and is not edited.
- (note) Read-only evidence reports 31628 regular files, 22445 canonical tracks, 6212 hidden-path files, 2933 images, 38 other artifacts, and no current symlinks.
- (decision) Only the canonical audio-extension policy in lib/sh/audio.sh defines track extensions; no player-specific extension list is allowed.
- (decision) An eligible player track is a regular non-symlink file with a canonical extension, outside dot-files and hidden directory components.
- (decision) Extension matching is ASCII case-insensitive; other path bytes remain literal, while ASCII control characters fail closed under the portable rule format.
- (decision) Nested normal directories are eligible; source symlinks and special files are ignored, hard-linked paths copy independently, and mounted descendants are treated as real descendants.
- (capability) CAP-1: Every player transfer uses one shared positive track-only payload definition, proven by matching sizing and transfer membership.
- (capability) CAP-2: Player Content synchronization converges the validated player root to exactly the checked album track snapshot and deletes all other destination content.
- (capability) CAP-3: Browser transfer converges each requested validated album destination to its exact track snapshot without claiming ownership of untouched player areas.
- (capability) CAP-4: The operator receives a success result only after sizing, transfer, flush, source-stability, and destination postcondition checks succeed; failures preserve checked paths and support retry.
- (decision) Sizing first creates an exact eligible-track snapshot; sizes and ordered rsync rules are derived from that same snapshot.
- (decision) Manifest and rule generation streams through secure temporary files rather than growing Bash arrays or rsync argv.
- (decision) Temporary artifacts must be created outside the library, player, repository, and database directory with restrictive permissions and checked writes.
- (decision) Player Content uses one library-root rsync with delete and delete-excluded; browser uses album-scoped rsync calls with the same deletion semantics.
- (decision) The rsync transfer disables source symlink and special-file payloads, ends its ordered allowlist with a catch-all exclusion, and preserves source-relative destination layout.
- (decision) A successful operation requires rsync exit zero, flush exit zero, an unchanged source path-type-size snapshot, and destination path-type-size equality for the owned scope.
- (decision) Player Content owns the whole validated destination root; browser owns only requested album subtrees and leaves other album trees unchanged.
- (decision) Browser sources must resolve strictly beneath the library root, all requested destinations must be unique, and browser adopts the full disjoint destructive-destination preflight.
- (decision) No automatic destination cleanup occurs on installation, selector entry, or policy-cache migration; cleanup begins only from explicit sync or transfer.
- (decision) The first successful global sync removes legacy images, hidden files, documents, caches, archives, playlists, unrelated files, and deselected albums.
- (decision) Existing cached selection sizes are versioned through app_meta and refreshed atomically under the new payload policy before they are trusted.
- (decision) Zero selection is valid after full safety preflight and empties the validated player root; a checked album with no eligible tracks has size zero and contributes no files.
- (constraint) Source-library content is never deleted, rewritten, renamed, or used for temporary artifacts.
- (constraint) Preserve destination safety checks, selection persistence, retry behavior, zero-selection behavior, display-only free-space reporting, and the current non-atomic destination failure model.
- (constraint) Temporary creation, traversal, sizing, rule generation, selection comparison, rsync, flush, or postcondition failure reports failure and never reports success.
- (constraint) Preserve Bash 5 and macOS/Linux portability, including GNU rsync and macOS-compatible openrsync behavior used by the portable design.
- (constraint) All automated verification uses temporary fixtures and never accesses a real music library, live database, or mounted player.
- (decision) Proposed ADR-0007 records the durable track-only destination policy; proposed ADR-0008 records the replaceable compiled file-backed rsync snapshot mechanism.
- (direction) The change map covers lib/sh/audio.sh, lib/sh/player.sh, lib/sh/sqlite.sh, bin/audlint-maintain.sh, bin/audlint.sh, focused tests, accepted architecture documentation, and the two proposed ADRs.
- (direction) Compare and reject negative filters, split include-from and exclude-from files, files-from alone, repeated per-album global synchronization, custom deletion, source-side filter files, and temporary symlink staging.
- (constraint) Capacity enforcement, transcoding, playlists, destination content checks outside explicit transfer, media probing for track identity, atomic destination swaps, and independent byte-integrity checks are outside scope.
