- (direction) Create R3.3 as the third bounded child of the existing R3 analyzer corpus and benchmark baseline; the parent R3 contract and R3.1 and R3.2 child contracts are authoritative and must not be redesigned.
- (decision) The child slug is r3-3-analyzer-work-instrumentation and the artifact is a standalone SPEC.md with no companion because the exact field and counting contract fits the kernel without a separate catalog.
- (decision) R3.3 applies only to the existing successful audlint-analyze --json structured boundary; the corpus runner consumes this evidence later and human-readable logs are never a data source.
- (capability) CAP-1 Analyzed-track evidence: the analyzer reports total emitted analyzed tracks and per-track instrumentation so later R3 benchmark work can aggregate actual analyzer work.
- (capability) CAP-2 Metadata-probe evidence: the analyzer reports total and per-tool ffprobe and soxi process invocation counts at album and track level.
- (capability) CAP-3 Decode-work evidence: the analyzer reports total decoder process invocations and splits them by segment versus full scope and by sox versus ffmpeg at album and track level.
- (capability) CAP-4 Decision-context preservation: structured output retains selected and initial strategies, fast-to-exact and segment-to-full fallback state, album and track confidence, decisions, and applicable fallback reasons without changing analyzer behavior.
- (constraint) Add top-level track_count, metadata_probe_count, metadata_probe_counts, decode_operation_count, decode_operation_counts_by_scope, and decode_operation_counts_by_tool fields to successful album JSON.
- (constraint) Add metadata_probe_count, metadata_probe_counts, decode_operation_count, decode_operation_counts_by_scope, and decode_operation_counts_by_tool to each emitted track object; album counters equal the sums of emitted-track counters and track_count equals the length of tracks.
- (constraint) metadata_probe_counts has exact nonnegative integer keys ffprobe and soxi; metadata_probe_count equals their sum at both album and track level.
- (constraint) A metadata probe is counted once when an ffprobe or soxi process is launched for metadata during the audlint-analyze --json command, including repeated calls, failures, and malformed results; availability checks and decoder processes are excluded.
- (constraint) decode_operation_counts_by_scope has exact nonnegative integer keys segment and full, decode_operation_counts_by_tool has exact keys ffmpeg and sox, and decode_operation_count equals each map's sum at both album and track level.
- (constraint) A decode operation is counted once when an external sox or ffmpeg PCM decode process is launched, including failed attempts, timeouts, and backend retries; in-memory reuse, FFT windows, channel passes, and decision recomputation do not count.
- (constraint) Segment decodes count under segment and a later whole-track decode counts under full, so segment-to-full fallback retains the segment work and adds the full work; auto fast-to-exact analysis over already decoded PCM adds no decode operation.
- (constraint) Existing per-track decode_operations and segment_probe_decode_operations retain their current successful logical-decode semantics; the new invocation counters are additive and may be larger when a decoder attempt fails before fallback succeeds.
- (constraint) The complete command boundary includes metadata probes launched by the Python analyzer and shared shell probing used before JSON is finalized; shared counting mechanics live in lib/py or lib/sh and bin/audlint-analyze.sh remains orchestration only.
- (constraint) Existing structured names remain the strategy and fallback authority: album_analysis_strategies and per-track analysis_strategy and initial_analysis_strategy; album and per-track auto_exact_fallback; album_strategy_fallback_to_full and per-track strategy_fallback_to_full.
- (constraint) Existing album_confidence and per-track analysis_confidence, statistical_confidence, decision_confidence, decision_reason, fallback_reason, analysis_strategy_reason, and applicable segment_probe decision, confidence, and fallback fields remain present and unchanged.
- (constraint) All existing target profiles, fake-upscale values, recode decisions, thresholds, analysis modes, strategy selection, fallback triggers, decode reuse, cache reads and writes, cache invalidation, serial execution, exit status, text output, and existing JSON meanings remain unchanged.
- (constraint) Focused temporary-fixture tests cover a normal track, failed-decoder backend retry, fast-to-exact reuse, segment-to-full fallback, and a multi-track album; stub process logs must equal structured counters and album totals must equal per-track sums.

- (assumption) The parent metric track_count means the count of per-track result objects in successful analyzer JSON, matching the parent definition of analyzed tracks in structured output rather than every input path that failed before a track result existed.

- (decision) The exact additive fields are track_count, metadata_probe_count, metadata_probe_counts with ffprobe and soxi keys, decode_operation_count, and decode_operation_counts with segment and full keys.
- (decision) Top-level probe and decode counters cover all external work launched during the successful JSON command, including work for an input that does not become a track result; they need not equal a sum of per-track fields because no new per-track invocation counters are added.
