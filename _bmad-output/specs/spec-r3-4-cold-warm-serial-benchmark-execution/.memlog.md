- (direction) Headless express mode selected because the user supplied the parent and three bounded child contracts, a complete R3.4 scope, explicit exclusions, and the requested standalone artifact.
- (decision) The child slug is r3-4-cold-warm-serial-benchmark-execution and the deliverable is a standalone SPEC.md plus a benchmark-results-schema.md companion for later fresh-context bmad-build.
- (direction) The R3 parent and R3.1 through R3.3 child contracts are authoritative; review found no conflict, so R3.4 consumes their validated manifest, immutable staging, accuracy evidence, and structured instrumentation without changing their semantics.
- (note) Brownfield inspection at commit 8b673bc on release/v1.4.0 found the legacy corpus runner still accepts direct paths and none of R3.1 through R3.3 is implemented; R3.4 therefore specifies required dependency contracts rather than treating planning output as behavior.
- (capability) CAP-1 Validated benchmark plan: maintainers can derive deterministic applicable tool and case work from the fully validated R3.1 manifest while retaining manifest order and identity.
- (capability) CAP-2 Isolated serial cold/warm execution: every tool and case iteration runs its own fresh R3.2 stage, executes cold then immediate warm with identical command inputs, and cannot warm another tool.
- (capability) CAP-3 Product-only monotonic timing: every executed sample measures only child-process launch through termination with a monotonic clock and excludes validation, staging, hashing, evidence parsing, verification, and cleanup.
- (capability) CAP-4 Complete raw benchmark evidence: the stable benchmark-results.json records reproducibility identity, exact sanitized commands, declared and observed cache state, exit and elapsed data, R3.2 verdict evidence, and applicable R3.3 counters, strategies, fallbacks, and confidence for every ordered sample.
- (capability) CAP-5 Safe exclusion and failure propagation: unproven converter paths are excluded without execution, while command, output, staging, mutation, evidence, or cleanup failures remain explicit errors and make the overall run nonzero.
- (decision) Applicable work is audlint-analyze for every analyzable manifest entry, audlint-value for analyzer and DR14-supported entries, cue2flac for declared CUE cases, any2flac for applicable PCM or lossy cases, and dff2flac for declared DFF cases.
- (decision) Execution order is manifest entry order, then fixed tool order audlint-analyze, audlint-value, cue2flac, any2flac, dff2flac, then iteration number, then cold followed by warm.
- (decision) A cold sample begins in a freshly verified stage containing only manifest-declared inputs and none of the generated Audlint, done-marker, DR, converter-cache, report, or output artifacts relevant to that command.
- (decision) A warm sample is the immediate identical second product invocation in the same stage after cold evidence capture, retaining all cold-generated stage and temporary-output artifacts and allowing no intervening product command or deliberate cache change.
- (constraint) Cold is application-cold only; operating-system page cache is uncontrolled, no privileged eviction is attempted, the limitation is recorded in every result, and R3.4 never calls a sample hardware-cold.
- (decision) Each tool-case-iteration cold/warm pair starts from a separate fresh stage outside the corpus root; additional iterations always create another stage, so no tool, case, or iteration inherits another pair's application artifacts.
- (constraint) Only one measured product command runs at a time and every supported internal worker control is forced to 1; current converter controls ANY2FLAC_ANALYZE_JOBS and DFF2FLAC_ANALYZE_JOBS are recorded as 1, and no unsupported worker knob is invented.
- (decision) The measured interval starts with time.monotonic_ns immediately before launching the product subprocess and ends immediately after that subprocess terminates and its pipes are drained; all manifest work, stage preparation, snapshots, hashing, cache inspection, JSON parsing, aggregation, publication, and cleanup are outside it.
- (decision) Cold and warm use the same executable, argv, working directory role, sanitized environment overrides, and temporary output role; only the retained application artifacts and declared cache_condition differ.
- (decision) Converter execution requires a focused temporary-fixture proof for the exact tool and command form that its source stage remains byte-for-byte and metadata identical and all outputs are confined to a separate temporary output root when supported.
- (decision) If no proven non-mutating read-only, plan-only, or dry-run form exists for a converter case, both planned sample conditions are recorded as excluded with executed false and a stable reason, and the product command is not launched.
- (constraint) A converter that passed the pre-execution gate still receives pre/post stage snapshots around each invocation; any observed mutation is an error that invalidates the run, never successful benchmark data or a retroactive exclusion.
- (decision) audlint-analyze and audlint-value run against R3.2 stages even though their application artifacts may change the stage; R3.2 source-subtree snapshots and cleanup remain mandatory outside timing.
- (decision) R3.3 counters and existing strategy, fallback, confidence, reason, and decision fields are consumed only from structured product output; audlint-analyze samples require them, while tools that do not expose them record null plus a stable unavailability reason rather than scraping logs or inferring work from time.
- (decision) Observed cache_state is one of hit, miss, bypass, or unsupported and is supported by recorded structured product evidence and before/after relevant artifact facts; runtime alone never establishes cache behavior.
- (decision) Every planned sample record preserves case, tool, iteration, cold or warm condition, applicability and executed state, exact sanitized command representation, serial settings, exit status, elapsed_ms, observed cache evidence, R3.2 verdict when applicable, R3.3 evidence when available, status, and stable reason code plus detail.
- (decision) A cold command error prevents its warm command because the retained state is not a valid warm baseline; the warm record remains ordered with executed false and error reason cold_failed. Every attempted command failure, malformed required structured output, safety failure, or cleanup failure makes the overall result nonzero.
- (decision) Converter exclusions are explicit and do not alone make the overall run nonzero; missing or failed required audlint-analyze or audlint-value execution is an error rather than a safety exclusion.
- (decision) All successful elapsed samples remain in raw execution order. Deterministic aggregates are secondary and group successful executed samples by the parent-required trust, category, case class, tool, cache condition, strategy, and fallback dimensions without replacing raw evidence.
- (decision) The result identity records schema version, UTC generation time, manifest SHA-256 and corpus revision, repository full commit and dirty boolean, OS and version, architecture, CPU identity and logical cores, memory, Bash, Python, NumPy, SoX/soxi, FFmpeg/ffprobe, DR14 and invoked Audlint tool versions where discoverable, analyzer settings, serial overrides, iteration count, and the OS page-cache limitation.
- (constraint) The only stable artifact R3.4 publishes is _bmad-output/benchmarks/r3-analyzer-corpus-benchmark-baseline/benchmark-results.json; it is deterministic UTF-8 JSON with two-space indentation, sorted object keys, LF endings, one final newline, and arrays retaining defined order.
- (constraint) Committed evidence sanitizes corpus and temporary absolute paths to manifest-relative identities and stable $STAGE and $OUTPUT role tokens while preserving executable, argv, working-directory role, and environment overrides exactly enough to reconstruct each invocation.
- (constraint) Tests use repository-owned temporary fixtures and stub product commands only; they never access /Volumes/Music/Tests, a real music library, live SQLite database, mounted sync destination, backup target, or destructive audio, tag, artwork, or conversion workflow.
- (decision) Focused verification covers cold stage emptiness, warm artifact retention, cross-tool and cross-iteration isolation, monotonic timing boundaries, no command overlap, serial environment enforcement, converter pre-gate exclusion without launch, converter runtime mutation failure, deterministic JSON structure and order, raw-sample retention, cache evidence states, and cold and warm failure propagation plus cleanup.
- (decision) Likely implementation files are bin/audlint-analyze-corpus.sh, lib/py/audlint_analyze_corpus.py and possibly a focused lib/py benchmark module; likely focused tests are test/test_audlint_analyze_corpus_smoke.py and a new test/test_audlint_analyze_corpus_benchmark.py, with existing audlint-value and converter smoke fixtures reused or tightened for exact safe command proofs.
- (direction) R3.4 excludes R3.1 inventory redesign, R3.2 staging or verdict redesign, R3.3 field or counting changes, performance or cache-policy changes, analyzer decisions or thresholds, parallelism, synthetic reproduction, baseline-summary.md publication, and R3A, R4, R5, or R6 work.
- (note) Wrapper-only content dropped: IDE tab state, escaped spaces, HTML nonbreaking-space entities, and the requested final-summary numbering are response ceremony rather than implementation behavior.
- (decision) No unresolved product question blocks R3.4; exact module splitting may follow repository conventions as long as the result schema and dependency boundaries remain unchanged.
